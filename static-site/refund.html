Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains how cancellations and refunds work for projects and services purchased from THV Technologies. Software development is custom work, so refunds are tied to the stage of work completed.

1. General Principle

Because we allocate dedicated developers, designers and marketers to your project, payments cover time and resources reserved for you. Refunds are therefore calculated based on work completed and costs already incurred.

2. Advance / Booking Payment

The initial advance confirms your project slot and covers discovery, planning and onboarding. It is non-refundable once the project has been kicked off.

3. Cancellation by the Client

  • Before kick-off: cancel within 48 hours of payment for a refund minus a 10% administrative fee and any payment-gateway charges.
  • During design stage: you pay for design work completed; the unused portion of milestone payments, if any, is refunded.
  • During development: you pay for all milestones completed or in progress, pro rata; no refund for delivered work.
  • After delivery / launch: no refund; defects are handled under warranty and support terms.

4. Cancellation by THV

If we cancel for reasons other than your breach, or are unable to deliver the agreed scope, we will refund payments for work not yet performed.

5. Digital Marketing & Subscriptions

  • Monthly retainers can be cancelled with 15 days’ written notice before the next billing cycle.
  • Fees for the current month and ad spend already used are non-refundable.
  • We do not offer refunds for lack of ranking, traffic or sales results as outcomes cannot be guaranteed.

6. Hosting, Domains & Third-Party Costs

Domain registrations, hosting, licences, app-store fees and similar purchases are made with third parties and are non-refundable.

7. Quality Issues

If a Deliverable does not meet the agreed scope, tell us in writing within 7 days of delivery. We will correct it at no extra cost; if we are unable to, a partial refund proportional to the non-conforming part may be offered.

8. How Refunds Are Processed

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7–14 business days. Bank or gateway fees may be deducted where applicable.

9. How to Request

Email your request with project name, invoice number and reason to admin@thvtechnologies.com. We will respond within 3 business days.
