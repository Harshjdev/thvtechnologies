Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what personal data THV Technologies collects, why we collect it, how we protect it and what choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact details: name, email, phone, company, when you fill a form or email us.
  • Project information: requirements, files, credentials and business details you share.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser, device, pages visited, referral source (via cookies and analytics).
  • Payment details: billing information handled by our payment processors; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Communications: messages, WhatsApp chats and call notes related to your enquiry.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to enquiries and provide quotes
  • To deliver, support and improve our services
  • To process payments and issue invoices
  • To send project updates and, with consent, newsletters or offers
  • To analyse website usage and improve user experience
  • To comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud

3. Legal Basis & Consent

We process personal data based on your consent, to perform a contract with you, to meet legal obligations and for our legitimate business interests. Where we rely on consent you may withdraw it at any time.

4. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies for essential functions, analytics and marketing. See our Cookie Policy for details and how to manage them.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with:

  • service providers such as hosting, email, analytics, CRM and payment processors acting on our instructions
  • professional advisers (legal, accounting, audit)
  • authorities when required by law or to protect rights and safety
  • a successor entity in a merger or acquisition

6. Client Project Data

Data and source material you give us for a project is used only to deliver that project, is covered by our confidentiality commitments and is returned or deleted on request after the project, subject to legal retention requirements.

7. Data Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above, and as required by tax, accounting and legal rules. Enquiry data not leading to a project is typically deleted within 24 months.

8. Data Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls, secure hosting and staff confidentiality obligations. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your data and will notify you of breaches as required by law.

9. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law (including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and where relevant GDPR) you may:

  • access and obtain a copy of your data
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • request erasure of your data
  • withdraw consent or object to certain processing
  • nominate someone to exercise rights on your behalf
  • lodge a complaint with the relevant authority

To exercise these rights, email us at the address below. We may need to verify your identity first.

10. International Transfers

Our service providers may process data in other countries. Where we transfer data abroad we take steps to ensure appropriate safeguards consistent with applicable law.

11. Children’s Privacy

Our website and services are not directed to children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Third-Party Links

Our site may link to external websites (e.g. social media, client sites). We are not responsible for their privacy practices; please review their policies.

13. Grievance Officer

For privacy concerns or complaints, contact our Grievance Officer at admin@thvtechnologies.com. We aim to acknowledge within 48 hours and resolve within 30 days.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above.
