This policy explains how THV Technologies uses cookies and similar technologies on our website, and how you can control them.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site work, remember preferences and understand how visitors use it.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential: required for core functions such as navigation, security and form submission.
  • Preference: remember your settings, such as language or theme.
  • Analytics: help us understand traffic and improve content (e.g. Google Analytics).
  • Marketing: measure ad performance and show relevant ads (e.g. Meta Pixel, Google Ads).

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by third parties such as analytics, advertising, embedded videos, fonts and chat widgets. These providers have their own privacy policies.

4. Managing Cookies

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may affect how the site works. You can also opt out of Google Analytics using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. Updates

We may update this policy as our use of cookies changes. See also our Privacy Policy.
